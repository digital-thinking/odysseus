GeoNames geographical database
https://www.geonames.org/

src/gallery_library/geodata/cities5000.tsv.gz is derived from the GeoNames export
"cities5000" (https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities5000.zip): the
name, position, country code and population of each town with 5,000 or more
people, without the entries for parts of towns (feature code PPLX).

Changes: columns reduced as above, coordinates rounded to three decimals,
rows sorted by country and name.
